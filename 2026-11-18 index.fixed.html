How it works

  • One door opens each day at 8am and closes at 8pm, in your own time zone. There are no games on Sundays.
  • Enter your full name and department before every game.
  • Questions are shared out so the people playing before and after you get a different set.
  • You get 30 seconds for each question. Puzzles have one short timer. You get one go per door.
  • A missed door stays closed. You can't go back to it.
  • Every game is worth 50 to 200 Nectar points. Harder games are worth more. Get at least one answer right to earn the minimum 50.
  • Winners are crowned on Saturday 5 December.
